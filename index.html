Jin-Seop Lee

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Sungkyunkwan University, working in the Information & Intelligence System Lab (IISLab), advised by Prof. Jee-Hyong Lee and co-advised by Prof. JinYeong Bak. Previously, I was a visiting researcher at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, where I worked with Prof. Boyang Albert Li and Prof. Jaehong Yoon. I received my B.S. degree from Sungkyunkwan University.

wlstjq0602@skku.edu

I aim to build a J.A.R.V.I.S.-like AI assistant, with a particular focus on proactiveness.

In the longer term, I am interested in integrating visual cues such as gaze into proactive AI, accounting for individual user personas, and developing agents that proactively identify and continually maintain the skills needed to assist users.

My research includes:

Proactive AI

Proactive Assistance, Intervention Timing, Personalized Guidance

Self-Evolving AI

Continual Learning, Learning from Feedback, Personalization

Video Understanding

Streaming Video, Egocentric Video, Procedural Understanding

Self-supervised Learning

Representation Learning, Contrastive Learning

Data-efficient Learning

Semi-supervised Learning, Weakly Supervised Learning, Domain Generalization
